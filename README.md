# Awesome iOS Privacy-First Apps

A curated list of iPhone and iPad apps that **keep your data on your device** — no account, no cloud upload, no tracking. Many are also **pay-once** (no subscription). The list mixes well-known privacy tools with smaller indie apps built around on-device processing.

> "Privacy-first" here means the core function works on-device, without sending your content to a server. Always verify current behavior in each app's App Privacy label.

> **Disclosure:** This list is maintained by Lumi Studio, developer of the Lumi Studio apps named below. Entries are factual and non-ranked; other developers' apps are included for context.

## Photos & Vault

- [Obscura](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1497262424) - Manual camera that keeps photos on-device.
- [PicClear](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6780223070) - Finds duplicate and similar photos with an on-device scan; nothing uploaded, review before deleting. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/picclear.html)
- [PhotoCream](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6781808054) - Film-look photo editing processed entirely on-device. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/photocream.html)
- [Unblurry](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6782275018) - Lumi Studio on-device sharpening and upscaling for soft-focus or low-resolution photos. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/unblurry.html)
- [Snapport](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6780575828) - Lumi Studio passport and ID photo maker with country-specific size templates, processed on the device. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/snapport.html)
- [Snapport Lite](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6792856304) - Lumi Studio free-to-start passport, ID and visa photo maker that works offline. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/snapportlite.html)
- [Mask My File](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6792850916) - Lumi Studio tool that finds private details in a file before you share it, so you can mask them and keep the rest readable. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/maskmyfile.html)

## Documents

- [Standard Notes](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1285392450) - Encrypted notes with a strong privacy stance.
- [ScanTo Pro](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6779977651) - Offline document scanner with OCR and Face ID lock; documents never leave your device. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/scanto.html)
- [CV Desk](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6781337213) - On-device ATS resume scoring and keyword matching; your CV isn't uploaded. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/cvdesk.html)
- [Sono Note](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6782139553) - On-device voice transcription, summary, and action items; no cloud account. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/sononote.html)
- [100 Notes Studio](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6798813048) - Lumi Studio offline notebook for handwriting, typed notes, PDF markup and page-attached audio, built around the iPad. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/notesstudio100.html)
- [AI Brief](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6791658210) - Lumi Studio tool that gathers screenshots, PDFs and notes into one organized brief before you hand context to an AI assistant. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/aibriefpack.html)

- [G+Money Lite](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6793436548) - Lumi Studio travel expense tracker with currency conversion and saved offline rates; one trip free, one-time unlock for more. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/gmoneylite.html)
- [HoursTag Lite](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794039979) - Lumi Studio free-to-start companion to HoursTag that shows a purchase in hours of your work, on-device with no account. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/hourstaglite.html)
